Why this combo

  • One gesture for everything: click → describe. Edits stack as layers you can toggle.
  • Most shareable output of the three combos ("my backyard → Mars").
  • Risks: needs an open segmentation/tracking model; edit quality and temporal stability over a full clip; crowded space.
  • Targets: Most Viral, LTX VFX, Open Weights.
