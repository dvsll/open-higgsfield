# Cinema Studio wireframe / mockup coverage

Research date: October 22, 2026. Scope is the documented corpus in the research brief, not an exhaustive authenticated audit of every current Higgsfield release. All 51 entries have a selectable design state in both Wireframe and Mockup modes.

The prototype has nine screens, feature inspection, editable mock draft values, selection, property expansion and job-state preview. It never calls generation APIs. One local generated still supplies sample artwork (studio/design-preview.png); no Higgsfield assets are shipped.
