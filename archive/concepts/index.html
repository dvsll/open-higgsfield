4. Style Board

LTX VFXOpen Weights

5. Storyboard

Story → shot list → generated boards → consistent multi-shot film.

Best OverallMost Innovative

6. Keyframes

Timeline editor: images/prompts at any time, LTX fills the motion between.

Open WeightsLTX VFX

7. Pinpoint Edit

Grok-style click-and-describe edits, open weights, propagated through video.

Most ViralOpen Weights

8. Drop-in 3D

Place a GLB model into real footage; it renders in with scene lighting and shadow.

Most InnovativeLTX VFX

9. Video → 3D

Phone walk-around → 3D scene → fly a new camera → render angles you never filmed.

Most InnovativeBest Overall

Risk: needs an open reconstruction model outside LTX.

10. Swap

Pick a person, prop or place in a video and replace it; motion stays.

Most ViralLTX VFX

LTX VFXOpen Weights

12. Motion Paint

LTX VFXOpen Weights

13. Street Remix

Most ViralLTX VFX

16. Agent Director

Brief → agent builds workflows via Comfy MCP, renders, self-critiques, retries.

Most Innovative

17. Style Tuner

Autoresearch loop: agent tweaks the workflow, renders a test clip, scores it against your style board, keeps what helps.

Most InnovativeLTX VFX
