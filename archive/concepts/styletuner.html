17. Style Tuner

Autoresearch-style loop (à la Karpathy): an agent proposes a change to the workflow, renders a short test clip, scores how close it is to your style board, keeps the change if the score goes up, repeats. You come back to a workflow tuned to your look.

How it fits

  • Plugs into Style Board (4) / Director (B): the output is a tuned workflow preset ("my look") used for every later shot.
  • Start with cheap knobs (prompt, strengths, refs, sampler settings). Actually training a LoRA per board is much slower; treat it as a stretch.
  • Strong Most Innovative story: "the app does its own research on your style while you sleep."

Open questions

  • Each run is a real LTX render — how many runs fit in a sane budget? Use short, low-res test clips.
  • Does the score match what humans see as "on style"? Embedding scores can be gamed (e.g. just copying colors).
  • The agent and scorer must be open models to stay Open Weights Only.
